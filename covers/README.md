# American Injustice — Cover Production Specs

Publication cover finalized from the approved front design on 2026-08-22.

## Paperback assumptions

- Trim: 6 × 9 inches
- Final interior page count used for cover geometry: 291 pages
- Interior: black-and-white
- Cover bleed: 0.125 inch on all outside edges
- Barcode: not embedded; reserve the lower-right back-cover area for KDP to place the ISBN barcode automatically

## KDP cream paper

- Spine formula: 291 × 0.0025 inch
- Spine width: **0.7275 inch**
- Full cover width including bleed: **12.9775 inches**
- Full cover height including bleed: **9.25 inches**

## KDP white paper

- Spine formula: 291 × 0.002252 inch
- Spine width: **0.655332 inch**
- Full cover width including bleed: **12.905332 inches**
- Full cover height including bleed: **9.25 inches**

The cream and white paperback cover files are not interchangeable because the spine widths differ.

## Ebook

The approved front cover has been prepared at 1600 × 2560 pixels for the Kindle cover image and embedded in the publication EPUB.

## Source discipline

No ISBN was invented or embedded. Back-cover factual claims are limited to material supported by the current manuscript and source archive. If the interior page count changes, the print-cover geometry must be regenerated before publication.
