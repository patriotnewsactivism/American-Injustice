## Description

*American Injustice* follows Matthew Oliver Reardon, writing as Don Matthews, through nearly a decade of arrests, prosecutions, civil commitments, banishment orders, public-records disputes, appeals, and federal civil-rights litigation across Mississippi, Texas, Utah, and Louisiana.

The account is reconstructed from court filings, certified transcripts, police and agency reports, laboratory records, public-record productions, recordings, livestreams, and Reardon’s contemporaneous documentation. The book follows the 2017 Lafayette County aggravated-stalking case and plea; the Crowder false-reporting prosecution; the 2021 Justice Court convictions and appeal; the 2022 MBI stalking prosecution and later revocations; the Galveston DWI investigation and toxicology dispute; Utah records litigation; U.S. Marshals alerts and the federal courthouse prosecution; New Orleans records disputes; and the appellate and civil rulings that followed.

Where the record contradicts Reardon’s recollection, this edition says so. Where a claim remains unresolved, it is identified as unresolved.

## About the author

**DON MATTHEWS** is the pen name of Matthew Oliver Reardon, a U.S. Marine Corps veteran, independent journalist, and founder and publisher of We The People News. *American Injustice* is his documentary memoir.
