ORDER GRANTING MOTION TO WITHDRAWAL AS COUNSEL, MOTION TO STAY EXECUTION OF JUDGMENT WITHOUT 
BOND AND MOTION TO EXTEND TIME TO APPEAL 
HAVING COME for hearing on the motion of counsel for Matthew Reardon after 
judgment being entered in this cause. the court being fully advised in the premises. does hereby order and adjudge as follows: 
That the honorable Mitchell O. Driskell. III. counsel for Matthew Reardon. is hereby 
allowed to withdrawal as counsel and his representation of Matthew Reardon is hereby terminated. 
That Matthew Reardon is indigent and by reason of poverty he cannot offer security 
required by law and is unable to give bond to appeal the judgment against him. Mr. Reardon is 
allowed to appeal, if he desires to do so. and execution of judgment will be stayed without the 
requirement of supersedeas bond. 
That Mr. Reardon shall have an additional ten (10) days beyond the current appeal deadline 
to file an appeal should he desire to do so.
