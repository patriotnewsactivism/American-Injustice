STATE OF MISSISSIPPI APPELLEE
MOTION FOR ENLARGEMENT OF TIME
WITHIN WHICH TO FILE BRIEF FOR APPELLEE
COMES NOW Appellee, through undersigned counsel, and moves
this Court to grant thirty (30) days, until January 6, 2022 within which
to file the Brief for Appellee in the above-styled and numbered cause.
I.
The Brief for Appellee is due December 3, 2021. No other extension
has been requested. Further, counsel for Appellee has other briefs that
require answering which prevents completion of this brief immediately.
Counsel requests the extension of time in order to fit within her calendar
of other briefs and their respective due dates.
II.
Counsel respectfully submits that the amount of time requested is
reasonable under the circumstances and is not made for purposes of
mere delay, nor is it the consequence of a lack of attention by the
undersigned.
WHEREFORE, PREMISES CONSIDERED, Appellee
respectfully moves this Court to extend the time within which to file its
brief until January 6, 2026.
