ORDER DENYING DEFENDANT'S MOTION TO WITHDRAW GUILTY PLEA 
Before the Court is Defendant's Motion to Withdraw Guity Plea filed pro se. The 
defendant seeks to withdraw the plea he entered before this Court on September 30, 2022 to the 
crime of aggravated stalking, Miss. Code Ann. Â§ 97-3-107(2). This Court sentenced the 
defendant to serve a term of five years with five years suspended and zero years to serve and five 
years of unsupervised probation. In addition, the Court banished the defendant from Lafayette 
County, Mississippi for five years. The Court allowed the defendant to remain at his current 
address located in Lafayette County, Mississippi for 45 days to provide him time to relocate to a 
residence outside Lafayette County. The Court forbade the defendant from having any contact 
with the victims of his crime of aggravated stalking. 
In support of his Motion to Withdraw Guilty Plea, the defendant now claims both counsel 
appointed to represent him were ineffective. He also now claims he is not guilty of the charged 
crime of aggravated stalking. 
Considering the totality of the circumstances and the standard of Stickland v. Washington, 
466 U.S. 668, 687 (1984), the Court finds the defendant has not made the requisite showing to

The defendant informed the Court he discussed the petition to plead guilty with counsel and

As to defendant's claims in his petition that suggest he is not guilty of the crime of 
aggravated stalking, these claims are belied by his responses during his plea colloquy. When the 
Court asked the defendant if he was guilty of the crime of aggravated stalking, the factual basis 
of which he was informed on the record, the defendant responded he was in fact guilty of the 
crime. The defendant informed the Court he understood the rights he would be giving up by 
pleading guilty. The Court found the defendant had freely, knowingly, and voluntarily entered a 
plea of guilty to the crime of aggravated stalking. 
The Court also notes the defendant submitted to a mental evaluation in this cause. 
Immediately prior to proceeding with defendant's plea hearing, this Court found the defendant 
legally competent based on the findings presented in the evaluation report, findings that were 
stipulated to by defendant's counsel.
