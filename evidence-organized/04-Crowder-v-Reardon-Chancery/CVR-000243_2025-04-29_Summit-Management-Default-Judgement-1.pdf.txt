submit this Motion for Default Judgment. In support thereof, the Plaintiff would show: 
Plaintiff Summit Management Services, LLC filed its Complaint on March 9, 2017. A copy 
of the Complaint is attached hereto as Exhibit 1. 
2. The Complaint was personally served upon Matthew Reardon on May 16, 2017, pursuant 
to Rule 4 of the Mississippi Rules of Civil Procedure. A copy of the executed proof of 
service was filed with the Clerk on June 16, 2017. 
3. The Defendant failed to appear, plead, or otherwise defend his interest in the lawsuit 
within the time allowed for a response. 
4. The Plaintiff requested an Entry of Default on June 16, 2017. In support if its request, 
counsel for the Plaintiff submitted an affidavit in support of Plaintiff's request for an entry 
of default testifying to the facts concerning service of process as stated above. The affidavit

infant or incompetent person, or in the military service purview of the Servicemembers 
Civil Relief Act, 50 U.S.C. App Â§ 521.

Judgment against the Defendant.
