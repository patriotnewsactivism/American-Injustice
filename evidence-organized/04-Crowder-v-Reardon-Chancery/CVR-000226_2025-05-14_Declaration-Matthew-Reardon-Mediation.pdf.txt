Declaration of Matthew Reardon Regarding Lawful Recording
and Disclosure of Mediation Audio
1. I, Matthew Reardon, participated in a mediation session on May 13, 2025 facilitated by
the Utah State Records Ombudsman and attended by a representative of the Morgan County
Attorney’s Office.
2. During the session, the Ombudsman requested that the meeting not be recorded. I
verbally affirmed that I was not recording.
3. Notwithstanding that affirmation, I did record the session pursuant to Utah Code § 77-
23a-4, which allows a party to a conversation to lawfully record without notifying other
parties.
4. No confidentiality agreement was signed prior to or during the mediation, nor was any
formal legal restriction placed on the discussion or its contents.
5. I have chosen to disclose a portion of the recorded session as a journalist, public interest
advocate, and constitutional rights activist. The statements made during the session pertain
directly to matters of public concern, including alleged violations of Utah’s Government
Records Access and Management Act (GRAMA), and potentially reveal efforts to suppress
accountability or transparency.
6. This disclosure is made pursuant to my First Amendment rights, and I assert that this is a
lawful and protected act of public interest journalism.
Executed this 14th day of May, 2025.
