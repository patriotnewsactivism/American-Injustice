Establish Paternity, Child Custody, and Child Support filed by Co-Plaintiffs Matthew Oliver

matter herein and of the parties hereto, and having been advised that the parties have reached an 
agreement hereby finds as follows: 
1. 
That on August 19, 2014, Plaintiff Phyllis Marie Crowder gave birth to a female 
child, namely Lydia Elise Reardon (the "Minor Child"); 
2.

County Road 422, Water Valley, Lafayette County, Mississippi, and has done so since on or

in any other proceeding concerning the custody of or visitation with the Minor Child;

6. 
7.
