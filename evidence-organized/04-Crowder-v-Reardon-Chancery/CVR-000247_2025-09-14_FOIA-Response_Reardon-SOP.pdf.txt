Dear Mr. Reardon,
This letter is in response to your Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) request of 12
April 2017 which was received by this office on 13 April 2017 and assigned file number
DON-USMC-2017-005333. Your request seeks a copy of armory standard operating procedures
(SOP) from Marine Corps Combat Training (MCT), East.
The enclosed document is provided in response to your request.
Based on information retrieved from MCT, East, the only changes made to the armory
SOP from 2007 to 2008 includes the deletion of paragraph 4004 (lined out). Procedural
changes made in 2015 are also included.
In view of the above, you may consider this to be an adverse determination that may
be appealed to the Judge Advocate General (Code 14), 1322 Patterson Avenue SE, Suite 3000,
Washington Navy Yard, DC 20374-5066. Your appeal, if any, must be postmarked within 90
calendar days for the date on this letter and should include a copy of your initial
request, a copy of this letter, and a statement indicating why you believe your appeal
should be granted. I recommend that your appeal and its envelope both bear the notation
“Freedom of Information Act Appeal.” You may also submit an appeal via your FOIA online
account.
You also have the right to seek assistance and/or dispute resolution services from
the Marine Corps FOIA Public Liaison, Ms. Sally Hughes, at hqmcfoia@usmc.mil or (703) 614-
4008, and/or the Department of the Navy FOIA Public Liaison, Mr. Christopher Julka, at
Christopher.a.julka@navy.mil or (703) 697-0031. You may also contact the Office of
Government Information Services (OGIS) for assistance and/or dispute resolution at
ogis@nara.gov or 1-877-684-6448. For more information online about services provided by
OGIS, please visit their website at https://ogis.archives.gov.
We have determined you to be an “All Other Requester” for the purpose of assessing

duplication free of charge but are responsible for the payment of any search and/or
