MOTION TO SEAL I.F.P AFFIDAVIT 
Comes now Before this court, Matthew Reardon, the Plaintiff/Appellant, whom hereby moves for an order by this court to seal the I.F.P Application/Affidavit of Poverty which was insisted upon by the clerk's office at the request of the Circuit Court Judge and then placed on file publicly accessible. Plaintiff/Appellant/Movant is of the position that his IFP status for appeal was already approve to proceed on appeal without the pre-payment of supersedes bond via the original trial judge, Chris Childers and that a copy of that order granting approval was offered to this court on December 27, 2021, which was refused by the Clerk's office on that particular day. On 1/7/2022 when filing a motion requesting a hearing date be set, Plaintiff/Appellant was then told that it was being mandated that he file a new I.F.P Affidavit/Application which Plaintiff/Appellant believed and still believes was an odd request on a matter already filed and active, and a matter which he was already given approval to proceed IFP by the court he is 
appealing from. Further, Plaintiff/Appellant asserts that the Circuit Clerk's office should have had an obligation to seal application due to privacy concerns into intimate matters attested to regardless, and that not sealing this 
particularly when taking into consideration the prior stated facts would create undue hardship through harassment and additional mental anguish through the public divulging of private information by this court on matters in which there should be an expectation of privacy. 
THEREFORE, for the above reasons stated, Plaintiff/Appellant moves this court to Seal the I.F.P 
Application/Affidavit of Poverty filed 1/7/2021 without further delay to prevent potential irreparable harm incurred by the Plaintiff/Appellant
