RESPONSE TO NOTICE OF HEARING 
COMES NOW Bela J Chain III, County Attorney for Lafayette County, Mississippi and 
files this response to the document entitle NOTICE OF HEARING ON ENTERED MOTIONS 
AND PETITION, issue by issue as follows, to wit: 
Motion to Alter or Amend Judgement Under Rule 59: 
1) The Circuit Court of Lafayette does not have jurisdiction in this matter. The 
document as filed is identified as being "In the Justice Court of Lafayette County, Mississippi" 
and referes to the criminal conviction of Matthew Reardon. Copies of Abstracts of the 
convictions of Matthew Oliver Reardon are attached hereto collectively as Exhibit "A 
"A"; 
2) This said motion was provided by the Clerk of The Lafayette County Justice to the 
presiding judge, The Honorable Chris Childers; 
3) Judge Childers denied the relief sought. Further commenting that the moving party 
cited authority to the Mississippi Rules of Civil Procedure which were inapplicable by Order 
dated 8 December, 2022. A copy of the Order of the presiding judge is attached as Exhibit "B"; 
4) That ruling on the motion of Matthew Reardon was not appealed and further the 
principals of res judicata apply and therefore it is final; 
5) The Mississippi Rules of Civil Procedure do not apply in criminal matters but rather 
the Mississippi Rules of Criminal Procedure are the applicable rules; 
D.C 
Motion to Set Hearing Date of De Novo Appeal: 
6) The Mississippi Rules of Criminal Procedure specifically Rule 29.1 provides the 
procedure for taking an appeal from matters in justice court. Specifically by filing certain 
specified items with the Circuit Court of the applicable county. Rule 29.1(b) sets out in specific 
detail what is necessary to perfect one's appeal; 
7) To this date Matthew Reardon has not so complied with Rule 29.1 by filing the 
required items with the Circuit Court of Lafayette County, Mississippi; 
8) As noted in the comments to said Rule and referring the Mississippi Rules of Appellate 
Procedure, Rule 4(e), pending post trial motions do extend the time for the taking of an appeal, 
Again assuming that the Motion for relief is proper; 
To that end, the Motion of Matthew Reardon is without merit for he is not before the 
Circuit of Lafayette County, Mississippi. Matthew Reardon's post trial motion has been 
previously ruled on by the Judge assigned in the Justice Court of Lafayette County, Mississippi. 
Further, no appeal for trial de novo has been perfected by compliance with the applicable 
rules, namely Rule 29.1 of the Mississippi Rules of Criminal Procedure. 
Respectfully submitted on this the 12th day of April, 2022.
