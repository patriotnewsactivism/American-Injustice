ON TUESDAY, MAY 30, 2017, CHIEF DEPUTY SCOTT MILLS AND I, MAJOR JOHN HILL, TOOK INMATE MATT REARDON TO CHANCERY COURT FOR HIS RESTRAINING ORDER HEARING THAT WAS FILED BY ROBYN AND RHEA TANNEHILL. THE HEARING WAS CONTINUED SO MATT COULD GET AN ATTORNEY TO REPRESENT HIM. JUDGE GLENN ALDERSON RESCHEDULED THE HEARING FOR JUNE 15, 2017, AND EXTENDED THE RESTRAINING ORDER UNTIL THE NEW HEARING DATE. 
AS CHIEF DEPUTY MILLS AND I WERE ABOUT TO ESCORT MATT FROM THE COURT ROOM, JUDGE'S CLERK SAMANTHA DAVIS WEATHERSBEE TOLD ME TO HOLD UP BEFORE WE LEAVE SO THEY COULD GET THE SIGNATURE ON THE NEW PAPERWORK. AS WE STARTED ESCORTING MATT FROM THE COURTROOM RHEA THEN BROUGHT UP THE MATTER UP TO JUDGE ALDERSON THAT MATT WAS ON THE JUNE AGENDA OF THE CITY OF OXFORD BOARD MEETING AND DID NOT WANT HIM THERE SINCE THE RESTRAINING ORDER WAS IN PLACE. MATT STATED HE SHOULD BE ALLOWED TO GO SINCE HE WAS ON THE AGENDA BEFORE THE RESTRAINING ORDER WAS IN PLACE. JUDGE ALDERSON ADVISED MATT HE WAS TO STAY AWAY AND IF HE SHOWED UP HE WAS TO BE ARRESTED. 
CHIEF DEPUTY MILLS AND I THEN WALKED MATT OUT OF THE COURTROOM. I HAD MATT GO SIT IN A CHAIR IN THE BACK HALLWAY (SOUTHEAST) SO HE WOULD NOT BE AROUND ANYONE LEAVING THE COURTROOM WHILE WE WAITED ON THE PAPERWORK THAT MATT HAD TO SIGN. CHIEF DEPUTY MILLS THEN WALKED BACK INSIDE TO CHECK ON THE PAPERWORK. 
RHEA CAME OUT THE COURTROOM DOOR THAT WAS THE HALLWAY MATT AND I WERE IN, NEAR THE JUDGE'S CHAMBER. MATT TOLD RHEA "FUCK YOU. YOU ARE A FUCKING LIAR". RHEA THEN STARTED WALKING TOWARD MATT WITH A WATER BOTTLE IN HIS HAND AND SAID "I'LL BASH YOUR HEAD IN". I THEN STEPPED IN FRONT OF RHEA AND TOLD HIM TO LEAVE. RHEA STARTED WALKING AWAY AND GOING AROUND THE CORNER WHEN MATT SAID SOMETHING ELSE (WHICH I DIDN'T HEAR). RHEA WALKED BACK AROUND THE CORNER AND SAID SOMETHING ELSE THAT I DIDN'T HEAR BECAUSE I WAS TELLING HIM TO LEAVE WHICH HE DID. 
WHEN CHIEF DEPUTY MILLS WALKED OUT OF THE COURTROOM, HE AND I THEN TRANSPORTED MATT BACK TO THE LCDC.
