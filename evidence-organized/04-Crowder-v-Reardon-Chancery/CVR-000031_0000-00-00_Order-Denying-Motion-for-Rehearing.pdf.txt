ORDER DENYING DEFENDANT'S MOTION FOR REHEARING OR IN THE ALTERNATIVE HIS NOTICE OF APPEAL 
This cause came before the Court on Defendant's above-stated motion. The Defendant 
moves this Court for rehearing on his revocation of post-release supervision, which the Court 
revoked at a hearing held November 3, 2022. The Court finds Defendant's request for rehearing 
is not well-taken and is hereby DENIED. 
The Court also finds Defendant's request to appeal this Order is DENIED. Orders 
revoking post-release supervision may not be appealed, neither may the denial of a motion to 
reconsider that revocation. See Massingille v. State, 878 So.2d 252, 254 (2004). Instead, 
complaints about the revocation may be made in proceedings for post-conviction relief. Id. 
Therefore, Defendant's Motion for Rehearing or in the Alternative his Notice of Appeal 
is hereby DENIED.
