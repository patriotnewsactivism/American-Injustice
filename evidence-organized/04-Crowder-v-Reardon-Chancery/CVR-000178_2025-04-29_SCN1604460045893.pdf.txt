MOTION FOR WRIT OF HABEAS CORPUS SEEKING BAIL REDUCTION 
COMES NOW, the Movant, Matthew Reardon, by and through counsel, Kristian A. 
McCray of the Trout Law Firm, PLLC, and makes this Motion for Writ of Habeas Corpus 
Seeking Bail Reduction, and for good cause shows the following: 
1. Movant is illegally confined and restrained of liberty in the Lafayette County 
Detention Center with bail in the amount of $150,000. 
2.Movant confinement is illegal because bail is excessive, oppressive and beyond the 
financial means of Movant or her family and loved ones, in violation of the Eighth and 
Fourteenth Amendments to the United Stats Constitution and Â§Â§ 26 and 29 of the Mississippi 
Constitution. 
3. Movant respectfully requests this Court, after receiving evidence, to reduce the amount 
of bail to a reasonable amount in order that Movant be granted opportunity to obtain release from 
incarceration. 
WHEREFORE, PREMISES, CONSIDERED, Movant prays that the Court issues a 
Writ of Habeas Corpus to the Sheriff of Lafayette County, Mississippi, commanding production 
of the person of Matthew Reardon before this court instanter, then and there to show cause, if 
any there be, why Movant should not be discharged from such illegal confinement, or that 
Movant be allowed bail in a reasonable amount.
