Comes now, the State of Mississippi, by and through its Assistant District Attorney Tiffany 
Kilpatrick, and moves this Court to revoke the probation and sentence in the above-styled and 
numbered cause, and hold Mr. Reardon in custody until such hearing, and in support thereof would 
show the following, to wit: 
I. 
Defendant Reardon pleaded guilty to AGGRAVATED STALKING, one count, on September 
30, 2022. The Court sentenced Defendant to Five Years, all suspended, and as condition of his 
unsupervised term of Five Years probation, Defendant be banished from Lafayette County with two 
exceptions: that he (1) be allowed to enter and exit Lafayette County and go directly to the residence 
where he was then lodged until November 15, 2022, and (2) to attend court proceedings or scheduled 
court business for which he would provide one day notice to the Lafayette County Sheriff's 
Department. 
II. 
Defendant immediately violated the terms of his sentence on both September 30, 2022, and 
October 3, 2022. Undersigned Assistant District Attorney contacted Defendant's counsel and asked 
that they both return to the courthouse wherein Circuit Court was in term so that the Court could 
remind and again explain to the Defendant that he was not permitted unauthorized movement about 
the City of Oxford or Lafayette County. No revocation proceedings were initiated. 
III.
