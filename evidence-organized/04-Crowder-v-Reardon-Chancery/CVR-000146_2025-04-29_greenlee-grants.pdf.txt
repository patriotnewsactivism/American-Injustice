ORDER
This matter comes before the panel of Wilson, P.J., Greenlee and Emfinger, JJ., on
the State’s motion for additional time to file its brief, which was due no later than
December 3, 2021. Although the State’s motion was four days late, the State has not
previously requested an extension and it is within this Court’s discretion to grant an
extension despite the expiration of the deadline. M.R.A.P. 26(b) cmt. The State’s request
is well taken.
THEREFORE, the State’s motion for time is granted. The State’s brief is due no
later than Thursday, January 6, 2022.
