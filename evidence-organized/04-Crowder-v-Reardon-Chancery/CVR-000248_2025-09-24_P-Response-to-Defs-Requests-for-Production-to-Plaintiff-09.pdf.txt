GENERAL OBJECTIONS
1. Scope and Relevance: Plaintiff objects to each request to the extent it seeks information
that is not relevant to any party’s claim or defense or is not proportional to the needs of the
case under Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 26(b)(1). Plaintiff further objects to the
instructions and definitions in Defendants’ Requests for Production to the extent they
purport to impose obligations broader than or inconsistent with those set forth in the
Federal Rules of Civil Procedure.
2. Overbreadth and Burden: Plaintiff objects to each request that is phrased in an overly
broad manner (including requests for “each and every” document or “any and all”
documents without reasonable limitation) or that is unduly burdensome. Where
appropriate, Plaintiff will construe each request as limited to materials within his
possession, custody, or control, and reasonably related in scope and time to the incident at
issue.
3. Privilege and Work Product: Plaintiff objects to each request to the extent it seeks
information protected by the attorney-client privilege, work-product doctrine, or any other
applicable privilege or immunity. Any inadvertent disclosure of privileged information is
not intended to waive and shall not constitute a waiver of any privilege. Plaintiff will not
produce privileged or work-product materials and will provide an appropriate privilege log
for any responsive documents withheld on that basis, as required by the Federal Rules.
4. Confidential or Private Information: Plaintiff objects to each request to the extent it
seeks private or confidential information not relevant to the claims, including but not
limited to personal identifying information and private medical or employment information
that is not reasonably calculated to lead to admissible evidence. Where requests seek such
information beyond what is at issue in this lawsuit, they are overly intrusive and not
proportional. Subject to and without waiving this objection, Plaintiff will produce relevant,
non-privileged records (such as medical or employment records) related to the injuries and
damages claimed in this case, while reserving all rights to redact or withhold information
that is private, irrelevant, or privileged.
5. Documents Equally Available to Defendants: Plaintiff objects to any request that calls
for documents or information already in Defendants’ possession or control, or equally
available to Defendants through their own records or public sources (including Defendants’
internal records, policies, or publicly available documents). To the extent a request seeks
such materials, it is unduly burdensome and duplicative. Plaintiff’s responses are made
with the understanding that he need not produce documents that Defendants already have
or can obtain from more convenient sources.
