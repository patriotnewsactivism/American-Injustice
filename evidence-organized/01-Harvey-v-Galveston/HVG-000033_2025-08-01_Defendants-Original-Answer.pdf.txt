DEFENDANTS’ ANSWER, DEFENSES, AND JURY DEMAND
Defendants City of Galveston, Texas, and Galveston fire department
Battalion Chief William Roy herein file their defenses, answer, and request for jury
trial in response to the allegations and claims in Plaintiff’s complaint [Doc. 1].
FIRST DEFENSE
1. Plaintiff failed to plead facts that show a plausible claim for relief.
SECOND DEFENSE
2. Battalion Chief Roy is protected by qualified immunity from liability and
unnecessary burdens of suit. Battalion Chief Roy reasonably performed his duties
under color of law in objective good faith, within the scope of discretionary
firefighting authority, and within the general scope of his employment by the City
of Galveston, Texas.
THIRD DEFENSE
3. The City of Galveston is protected from suit and liability by governmental
immunity.

FOURTH DEFENSE
4. The Plaintiff failed to demonstrate any basis for waiver of the City’s
governmental immunity, including but not limited to the requirements under TEX.
CIV. PRAC. & REM. CODE §§ 101.101, 101.021, 101.055, 101.057, 101.062.
FIFTH DEFENSE
5. Plaintiff’s conduct was the sole cause of the occurrence which forms the basis
of the lawsuit, Plaintiff’s alleged harm, and Plaintiff’s claimed damage.
SIXTH DEFENSE
6. The City’s liability under Texas law is barred or limited by the provisions of
TEX. CIV. PRAC. & REM. CODE §§ 101.023, 101.024, 102.002, 102.003, and Chapter
33 of the TEX. CIV. PRAC. & REM. CODE.
SEVENTH DEFENSE
7. Plaintiff failed to use reasonable diligence to mitigate damages Plaintiff
claims.
EIGHTH DEFENSE
8. Battalion Chief Roy asserts his rights under the Fourteenth Amendment and
maintains that punitive damages are unconstitutional in this case to the extent they
are assessed:
a. In an amount left to the discretion of the judge and/or jury;
b. In any decision representing less than a unanimous verdict;
c. Where underlying culpability is not assessed upon a finding of guilt
beyond a reasonable doubt;
d. Where the standard of guilt is not clearly defined; and/or

ANSWER
9. Subject to the foregoing defenses, the Defendants answer Plaintiff’s claims
and allegations consistent with Rule 8 of the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure,
under which Defendants deny every claim and Defendants deny each factual
allegation in Plaintiff’s complaint except those Defendants expressly admit herein.
10. Plaintiff filed a suit against Defendants. Defendants deny the remainder of
the allegations in ¶¶ 1 and 2 of the complaint.
11. Robert Harvey is the plaintiff in this lawsuit. Defendants lack sufficient
knowledge and information to know Plaintiff’s residence and citizenship.
Therefore, Defendants deny the allegations in ¶ 3 of the complaint.
12. Battalion Chief Roy was employed by the City of Galveston, Texas, during
the occurrences which form the basis of the lawsuit. Battalion Chief Roy reasonably
performed his duties under color of law in objective good faith, within the scope of
discretionary firefighting authority, and within the general scope of his
employment by the City of Galveston, Texas. Defendants deny the remainder of the
allegations in ¶¶ 4-6 of the complaint.
13. This Court has jurisdiction over Plaintiff’s claims and venue is proper in this
Court. Defendants deny the remainder of the allegations in ¶¶ 7-8 of the complaint.
14. On June 22, 2024, Plaintiff and his friend failed to yield the right of way to,
or heed the warnings provided by, the authorized emergency fire vehicle Battalion
