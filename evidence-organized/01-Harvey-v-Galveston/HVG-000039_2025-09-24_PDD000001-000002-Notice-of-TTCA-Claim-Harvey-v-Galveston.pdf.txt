Factual Circumstances:
In the early morning of June 22, 2024, Mr. Harvey and his friends were walking
southbound on the 61st Street sidewalk in Galveston, Texas. Mr. Harvey was staying safely on the
sidewalk away from traffic. Chief Williams was parked in the GFD Truck on the western shoulder
of 61st Street. As Mr. Harvey and his friends walked on the pedestrian right-of-way past the GFD
Truck, Chief Williams negligently blasted his siren multiple times for no discernible reason,
injuring Mr. Harvey’s eardrums. Then Chief Williams accelerated his truck into the pedestrian
walkway where Mr. Harvey was standing and hit Mr. Harvey with the GFD Truck.
Nature and Extent of Harm:
As a result of Chief Williams’s negligence and GFD’s negligent entrustment of the GFD
Truck to Chief Williams, Mr. Harvey was injured in the knee, arm, shoulder, neck, and ear. Mr.
Harvey was checked out by EMS personnel on the scene and then went to a hospital later that day
to be treated for his immediate injuries. Over time, the pain from his injuries has become more
severe. He has since begun treatment for persistent pain, including in his shoulder, back, and neck,
and damage to his ears that has caused a loss of hearing.
Witness Information:
In addition to Mr. Harvey, Bruce Redman and Paul Santucci were at the scene and
witnessed the incident. Mr. Harvey also believes multiple videos exist of the incident. Further
information can be found in City of Galveston Police Department Incident Report 24-003697.
