Application For writ of Habeas Corpus seeking discharge from Illegal confinement, and for good cause shows the following:

the Sheriff of Galveston County, Texas on a fugitive from Justice warrant. 
by

Right

without due process of Law. 
1 
5) There exists no Statutory Authorization to hold a person under a Fugitive from Justice Warrant for more than 90-Days absent a Governor's Warrant (TX Code crimproc Article 51, 13 Sections 15 and 17). Therefore a person arrested under a fugitive warrant or Committed after a warrantless arrest must be discharged after the expiration of the 90-Days if no Governor's warrant has been issued by then (TX code crimproc 51.07) Citing [Ex parte Steel, 155 Tex crim 93, 230 (1950)] 
6) More than 130-Days have now elapsed since Defendant was arrested and committed to the Galveston County Jail, and No Governor's Warrant has issued; Thus Mandating Discharge of the fugitive from Justice Warrant as well as the Defendant From Custody Associated with such warlant. 
7) As such, Defendant's confinement and Restraint is Illegal both Statutorily and constitutionally, in violation of the 4th 5th 6th and 14th Amendments to the US Constitution, Article 1 Sections 9, 10, and 19 of the Texas Constitution, and Articles 51.04, 51.05, 51.0%, and 51,13 of the Texas Code of Criminal Procedure
