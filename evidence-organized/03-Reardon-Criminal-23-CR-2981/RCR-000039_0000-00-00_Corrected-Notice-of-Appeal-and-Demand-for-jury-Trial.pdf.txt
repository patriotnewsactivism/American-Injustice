CORRECTED NOTICE OF APPEAL AND DEMAND FOR JURY TRIAL 
COMES NOW plaintiff/appellant Matthew Reardon, by and through court-appointed 
counsel, and files his corrected Notice of Appeal and Demand for Jury Trial pursuant to Rule 
29.1(c) of the Mississippi Rules of Criminal Procedure as follows: 
1. On December 3, 2021, Matthew Reardon, unrepresented by counsel at the time, 
filed his "Motion to Alter or Amend Judgment Under M.R.C.P. 59(e), or in the Alternative, to 
Vacate Judgment Entered Pursuant to M.R.C.P. 60(b)(1, 2, 4, 6)." Mr. Reardon intended for this 
pleading to also serve as his Notice of Appeal of criminal convictions in the Justice Court of 
Lafayette County. According to Mr. Reardon, he later attempted to file a pleading styled "Notice 
of Appeal" but was not allowed to file that pleading. 
2. On Tuesday, April 12, 2022, this Court held a hearing on the pending Motions and 
issues of this matter. The Court stated that it appeared that the Motion to Alter or Amend did not 
comply with the requirements of the Mississippi Rules of Criminal Procedure for a proper notice 
of appeal from Justice Court, but the Court did not make a ruling nor identify the nature of any 
deficiency. A M.R.Cr.P. 29.1(c) written notice of deficiency has not been served on Mr. Reardon. 
This corrected Notice of Appeal is filed pursuant to M.R.Cr.P. 29.1(c) to preemptively correct any
