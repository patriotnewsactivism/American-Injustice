SOURCE OF ACTIVITY: On February 21, 2021, at 0154 hours, Deputy Kandi Beavers and I, Deputy Jacob Williford observed a vehicle with one headlight travelling eastbound on Jackson Ave. Matthew Oliver Reardon (33 y/o W/M) was subsequently arrested for driving under the influence of a controlled substance, expired license tag, and improper equipment. 
INVESTIGATION: While travelling westbound on Jackson Ave, I observed what appeared to be a black 2013 Kia driving eastbound with only one headlight illuminated. Deputy Beavers and I turned around to initiate a traffic stop on the vehicle. 
The vehicle turned onto Hathorn Rd. I then ran the tag and it came back to a black 2013 Kia (MS tag DAM 9526). I notified Dispatch my intention to make a traffic stop near the Ole Miss Softball Field. 
I approached the driver's side window, introduced myself and the department I represented. I notified Matt the reason for the stop - one illuminated headlight. I then smelled the odor of what I believed to be, through training and experience, burned Marijuana. I asked Matt if there was anything illegal inside the vehicle. He advised there was not. I requested he step out of the vehicle. 
Once Matt was outside of the vehicle, I requested his wife step out as well and stand at the front of the vehicle. I searched the vehicle but was unable to locate any contraband. Matt advised the odor stemmed from his smoking marijuana approximately an hour before the traffic stop. Matt requested a supervisor. Shortly afterwards, LT John Dukes arrived on 
scene.

"Sallyport" to perform Deputy Beavers' tests. I then allowed his wife to take the vehicle from
