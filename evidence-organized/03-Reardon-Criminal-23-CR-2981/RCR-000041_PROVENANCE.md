# Provenance correction — RCR-000041

The OCR source currently stored as:

`RCR-000041_0000-00-00_Matthew-Reardon-Notes.pdf.txt`

is misleadingly named as to authorship.

On August 27, 2026, Matthew Reardon identified the handwriting in the underlying June 28 page as **Mitchell Driskell's** handwriting.

The underlying page is dated `6/28`, is headed `Matt Reardon`, and contains shorthand notes concerning the 2022 aggravated-stalking matter, the July 6 preliminary/bond hearing, the City Hall encounter, a stated First Amendment-audit purpose, recording devices, and defense assertions including that Reardon had not asked for the person, had not grabbed the knob, and had not tried to go anywhere.

This provenance correction establishes who wrote the notes. It does not, by itself, establish the truth of every factual proposition Driskell recorded. In manuscript use, the page should be described as Mitchell Driskell's contemporaneous handwritten notes and distinguished from an independent judicial, investigative, or video finding.

The legacy OCR filename is preserved to avoid breaking existing references.
