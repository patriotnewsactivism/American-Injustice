MOTION TO WITHDRAWAL AS COUNSEL, MOTION TO STAY EXECUTION OF JUDGMENT WITHOUT BOND AND MOTION TO EXTEND TIME TO APPEAL 
COMES NOW counsel for Defendant Matthew Reardon and moves the court as follows: 
1. 
Upon Mr. Reardon's request for counsel, undersigned counsel was assigned to represent Mr. Reardon in these misdemeanor cases outside of the County's regular public defense service by Circuit Court Judge Kelly Luther. Counsel has confirmed with Judge Luther that this special appointment of counsel does not extend to appeal. Counsel moves for permission to withdraw as counsel as required by M.R.A.P. 6(b). 
2. 
Mr. Reardon has been deemed indigent by the court. Mr. Reardon remains indigent and, by reason of his poverty, is unable to give bond or other security as required by law to appeal the judgments against him. Mr. Reardon moves to stay execution of judgment without supersedeas bond. If a hearing is necessary on this issue, counsel requests that execution of judgment be stayed until such hearing and for ten (10) days following the trial court's ruling after hearing pursuant to M.R.A.P. 8(b)(5). 
3. 
Out of an abundance of caution and due to the court's judgment being filed on Wednesday, November 24, 2021, the day prior to Thanksgiving, counsel moves for an extension of time for Mr. Reardon to proceed pro se with an appeal if he desires to do so. 
WHEREFORE, PREMISES CONSIDERED, counsel for defendant requests the relief identified above and for any additional or alternate relief the court deems appropriate.
