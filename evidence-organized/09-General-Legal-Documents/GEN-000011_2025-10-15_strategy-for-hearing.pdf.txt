1. The Introduction:
o "Good morning, Your Honor. Matthew Reardon, appearing on my own behalf."
2. The Purpose:
o "I have received the Court's notice and I am prepared to discuss the scheduling
order and all pending matters."
3. Raise Your Key Points (get these on the record!):
o "Your Honor, before we set deadlines, there are two key issues that will affect the
schedule that I need to bring to the Court's attention."
o "First, is the matter of service on two defendants in Mississippi, Hoby James and
Kandis Beavers. I have encountered significant difficulties and may require the
Court's assistance to effect service, which will impact discovery deadlines."
o "Second, I intend to file an Amended Complaint to add critical facts that have
come to my attention since the initial filing. This will likely affect the defendants'
current Motion to Dismiss and should be addressed before we set a final deadline
for that response."
❗ Potential Issues to Deal With (Reading the Defense) ❗
Be ready for these topics to come up. Here's how to handle them!
 The Consent to a Magistrate Judge: The notice specifically says to be ready for this!
o What it is: This form lets you agree to have Magistrate Judge Edison handle
EVERYTHING in your case, including the trial and final judgment. If you
consent, any appeal goes straight to the Fifth Circuit Court of Appeals.
o Your Decision: This is 100% your call.
 Pro-Consent: Magistrate judges often have more time and can move
cases along faster. It can be very efficient.
 Anti-Consent: You have a right to have an Article III District Judge (like
Judge Brown) preside over your trial and enter the final judgment.
o What to Say: "Your Honor, I have reviewed the consent form. At this time, I
would like to [state your choice: 'consent to proceed before you' OR 'reserve my
right to proceed before the District Judge']."
 The Docket Control Order (DCO): This is the main event! The judge will want to set
deadlines for everything. Be ready with a calendar!
o Potential Deadlines: Amending Pleadings, Adding Parties, Disclosures, Expert
Witnesses, Discovery Completion, Dispositive Motions (like Summary
Judgment).
o Your Stance: Argue for more time, not less! Say, "Your Honor, given the
complexity of the case, the interstate conspiracy allegations, and the issues with
service in Mississippi, I believe a discovery period of at least [suggest a number,
e.g., 8-10 months] is necessary to adequately prepare."
❗ Things to BRING FORWARD (Your Offensive Plays) ❗
Be proactive! Don't wait to be asked about these. You bring them up!
 Service Issues on MS Defendants: This is CRITICAL. It stops the clock on them.
o Say this: "Your Honor, regarding service on Deputies James and Beavers, the
Lafayette County Sheriff's Department has been uncooperative. I will likely need
to file a motion for assistance from the Court to ensure they are properly served,
and I wanted the Court to be aware of this delay."
 Your Amended Complaint: This is your #1 priority! It can make their current Motion to
Dismiss moot.
o Say this: "Your Honor, because I intend to file an Amended Complaint, I'd
request that the Court set a deadline for me to file that amended complaint, and
then set a new briefing schedule for any motions to dismiss afterward. It would be
most efficient to address the amended complaint rather than the current one."
 Initial Discovery Needs: Briefly hint at what's coming.
o Say this: "Your Honor, just for purposes of scheduling, I want to note that
discovery in this case will be extensive, as I will need to seek documents from the
City of Galveston regarding their policies, training, and supervision to support my
Monell claim."
❗ Things to HOLD BACK ON (Avoiding Penalties) ❗
What you DON'T do is just as important as what you DO!
 DO NOT ARGUE THE MERITS: Do not get into a debate about the Motion to
Dismiss. Today is NOT the day to argue about whether Officer Osteen is a liar or if you
were wronged. The judge will cut you off. If it comes up, just say, "Your Honor, I will
address all of those arguments in my written response, which I believe should be filed
after I've had an opportunity to amend my complaint."
 DO NOT GET EMOTIONAL: Stay calm, cool, and professional. You are a serious
advocate. Let the facts in your complaint do the emotional work.
 DO NOT BE COMBATIVE: With the judge or opposing counsel. Be firm and
confident, but always respectful. Use "Your Honor" and refer to the other side as
"opposing counsel" or "counsel for the City."
"Your Honor, regarding UTMB's motion to stay based on sovereign immunity, in the interest of
streamlining this litigation, I am willing to amend my complaint to seek only declaratory and injunctive
relief against UTMB as an institution. This would resolve the sovereign immunity issue while allowing

stay and allow all claims to proceed on the merits."
"Your Honor, to efficiently resolve UTMB's motion to stay, I propose two paths forward. I am willing to
either amend the complaint to seek only declaratory relief against the institution, OR I can amend it to
name the responsible officials in their individual capacities. I am prepared to proceed either way the
Court and opposing counsel believe is most appropriate to keep this case moving forward."
"Your Honor, to assist the Court and expedite today's conference, I have prepared a proposed timeline
for the key deadlines. My primary request is for 30 days to file the amended complaint, which I believe
will clarify the issues and streamline the rest of the litigation. After that, I propose a 10-month discovery
period to account for the interstate nature of the case."
"Your Honor, allowing me to amend the complaint now will be the most efficient path forward. It will
ensure that the defendants' motion to dismiss targets the operative pleading, preventing the need for
multiple rounds of briefing and saving the Court's time."
"Your Honor, addressing the service issues in Mississippi now is critical for an efficient
