II. 
MOTION TO DISMISS 
7. As set forth in the Agreed Order attached hereto as Exhibit "C," Phyllis Marie 
Crowder agreed to dismiss her 2020 Justice Court Complaint against Defendant. 
8. The Agreed Order was signed by Ms. Crowder, her counsel, the Defendant and the 
Honorable Robert Q. Whitwell. The Agreed Order was filed with the Chancery Court. 
9. The Agreed Order should be respected and enforced by this Court. Ms. Crowder 
is prohibited by agreement from pursuing the 2020 charges. The Mississippi Supreme Court has 
explained that the law favors the settlement of disputes by agreement of the parties. McManus v. 
Howard, 569 So.2d 1213, 1215 (Miss. 1990). See also Welsh v. Mounger, 883 So.2d 46, 48 (7) 
(Miss.2004) (finding that the law favors settlement of disputes). They are contracts, made by the 
parties, upon consideration acceptable to each of them, and the law will enforce them. East v. East, 
493 So.2d 927, 932-33 (Miss.1986). Courts will not rewrite them to satisfy the desires of either 
party. Travelers Indem. Co. v. Chappell, 246 So.2d 498, 510 (Miss.1971). With regard to the 
property of the parties, this is a strong and enforceable rule with few, if any, exceptions. Osborne 
v. Bullins, 549 So.2d 1337, 1339 (Miss.1989); Morris v. Morris, 541 So.2d 1040 (Miss.1989). A 
"chose in action," or the right the right to initiate Court proceedings, is personal property. Garrett 
{D1785347.1} 
v. Gay, 394 So.2d 321, 322 (Miss. 1981). Ms. Crowder's agreement to dismiss the 2020 charges 
must be enforced. 
10. 
Ms. Crowder is also prohibited from pursuing the 2020 charged by the doctrine of judicial estoppel. The doctrine of "[j]udicial estoppel is designed to protect the judicial system 
and applies where 'intentional self-contradiction is being used as a means of obtaining unfair 
advantage in a forum provided for suitors seeking justice." "Kirk v. Pope, 973 So.2d 981, 991 
(Miss.2007). Judicial estoppel arises when one party asserts a position contrary to one taken 
in prior litigation. Clark v. Neese, 131 So. 3d 556, 560 (Miss. 2013). Judicial estoppel prohibits 
a party from asserting a position, benefitting from that position, and then, when it becomes more 
convenient or profitable, retreating from that position." Id. In Kirk v. Pope, this Court held that there are three elements of judicial estoppel. Kirk, 973 So. 2d at 991. A party will be judicially estopped from taking a subsequent position if (1) the position is inconsistent with one previously taken during litigation, (2) a court accepted the previous position, and (3) the party did not inadvertently take the inconsistent positions. Id. All of the elements are met herein. Ms. Crowder 
is judicially estopped from pursuing the 2020 charges that she promised to and was ordered to 
dismiss. 
11. 
Allowing Ms. Crowder to pursue those charges would be fostering and allowing Contempt of Court. Legally and equitably, Ms. Crowder should not be permitted to change her position and be in violation of an Agreed Order, 
12. 
The Criminal Complaint from 2020 must be dismissed.
