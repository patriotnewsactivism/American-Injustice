I, Mickey Avent, the Justice Court Judge assigned to the above styled cases, and in accordance with the Code of Judicial Conduct and as otherwise required by law, hereby recuse myself from hearing these cases. My reasons for recusal are set forth below: 
Mr. Reardon has made several appearances in the justice court office requesting to speak to me. I have told him numerous times it is not allowed for me to talk with him and he still insists. MOTION TO QUASH SUBPOENA DUCES TECUM 
Comes now Bela J Chain III, County Attorney for Lafayette County, MS, and files this his 
motion to quash the subpoena duces tecum issued at the request of Matthew Reardon, and in 
support will show the following: 
1. The issuance of a subpoena duces tecum is governed by Rule 33 of the Mississippi 
Rules of Criminal Procedure and as such requiring the prosecution to produce documents at a 
time and place other that at the hearing of same is disallowed without a hearing if seeking 
production otherwise. 
2. Portions of the subpoena duces tecum seeks video of the interior of the Lafayette 
County Detention center where individuals are held while being booked. Disclosure of the video 
evidence would cause irreparable harm as to the safety and security of the detention center. 
3. The subpoena further seeks matters that have no nexus to the criminal charges at bar in 
this instance. 
In an effort to comply with what is perceived by the undersigned as a request for discovery in a criminal prosecution pursuant to Rule 17.10 of the Mississippi Rules of Criminal Procedure the undersigned is providing in this motion the following items (copies not filed with 
the court): 
a. copy of the video of the exterior of the Lafayette County Detention Center where the 
event which lead to the criminal charges occurred. 
b. narrative reports of the officer which also identify witnesses to the events which lead 
to the criminal charges. 
c. copies of photographs which may be used by the prosecution at the criminal trial.
